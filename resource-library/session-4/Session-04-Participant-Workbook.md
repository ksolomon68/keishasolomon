# Session 4: Automation pipeline and recovery plan

No-Code Automation & Business Pipelines

## Your outcome

Build or simulate one intake-to-routing workflow with a clear human approval boundary and a safe response to failure.

### Before you begin

Choose a small intake or routing process. Use a test workspace and fictional submissions; identify any action that would send a message or alter real records.

### Working boundaries

Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

### What to keep

Keep a copy of your inputs, outputs, decisions and revisions. Browser-only drafts may not follow you to another device. Upload the completed resource or share a permitted link through your participant dashboard.

### Course coaching

Each cohort participant may request one remote coaching session during the course. Request it inside the platform; your instructor confirms the time. Use the same conversation for time changes.

## Lab guide

Follow your instructor or work through these steps using approved tools. A document or manual simulation is acceptable when a connected tool is unavailable; label its status honestly.

### Step 1

Plan the smallest pipeline: trigger → input checks → draft → human approval → action → destination verification. Agree on expected outputs before connecting tools.

### Step 2

Use a test workspace or a manual simulation. Add checks for missing fields, unsupported claims, and duplicate inputs; block the action until a human approves the exact draft.

### Step 3

Test a valid request, an invalid request, a duplicate, and a partial failure. Compare the destination records with the intended actions before retrying anything.

### Worked example — fictional / illustrative

Fictional example: a form creates one sandbox lead row and drafts an acknowledgement. A person approves the exact text before any send. Replaying the same request must not create a second lead. A manual simulation is acceptable when tool access is unavailable; label it honestly.
