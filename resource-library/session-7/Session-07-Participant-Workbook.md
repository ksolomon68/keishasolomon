### Working boundaries

Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

### What to keep

Keep a copy of your inputs, outputs, decisions and revisions. Browser-only drafts may not follow you to another device. Upload the completed resource or share a permitted link through your participant dashboard.

### Course coaching

Each cohort participant may request one remote coaching session during the course. Request it inside the platform; your instructor confirms the time. Use the same conversation for time changes.

## Lab guide

Follow your instructor or work through these steps using approved tools. A document or manual simulation is acceptable when a connected tool is unavailable; label its status honestly.

### Step 1

Tell the story: problem, solution, test result, and remaining risk.

### Step 2

Run a two-minute dry pitch and capture one challenge from a peer.

### Step 3

Bundle the artifact, operating guidance, evidence, and a 30/60/90-day adoption plan.

### Worked example — fictional / illustrative

Illustrative example: three trials suggest an 8-minute saving per comparable task, but one quality defect remains. The recommendation is a limited pilot with a named reviewer, not organization-wide deployment or guaranteed ROI.
