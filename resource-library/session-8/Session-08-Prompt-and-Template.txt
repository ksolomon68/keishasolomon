Session 8: Five-minute showcase and next-step guide
THE AI EXECUTIVE SANDBOX — EVOBRAND Concepts

PURPOSE
Demonstrate the problem, working solution, observed result and next decision in five minutes. Celebrate useful learning, including a well-evidenced decision not to deploy.

BEFORE YOU PASTE
Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

STARTER PROMPT
Turn this participant-supplied evidence [evidence] into a five-minute demo outline: problem, live workflow, observed result, limitation and next decision. Preserve uncertainty and sample counts. Do not invent savings, testimonials, approvals or certificate eligibility. Suggest a short fallback walkthrough if the live demo fails.

REUSABLE WORKING TEMPLATE
0:00-0:40 — problem, user and original effort
0:40-2:40 — show one complete workflow and human check
2:40-3:40 — measured result, method and sample count
3:40-4:30 — limitations, governance and ownership
4:30-5:00 — next decision and concrete ask
Backup demonstration location
Peer feedback: strongest evidence / unanswered question
My next step / owner / review point
What I will continue, change or stop

TEST CASES
1. Live demo: use fictional or approved examples and show the actual output and human check.
2. Fallback: prepare screenshots or a walkthrough in case the tool, connection or account is unavailable.
3. Challenge: answer a question about limitations, review effort or who will own the workflow after the course.

TRANSFER CHALLENGE
Run once with a timer and once using only your fallback. Finish within five minutes and keep one clear action for after the course.

SAVE AND REVIEW
Keep your own copy and follow the workbook submission instructions. Do not include passwords, private records or secret URLs.
