Session 5: Brand voice and 30-day content plan
THE AI EXECUTIVE SANDBOX — EVOBRAND Concepts

PURPOSE
Create a voice playbook, adapt one supported idea into three channels, and plan a realistic 30-day editorial window without assuming daily publication.

BEFORE YOU PASTE
Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

STARTER PROMPT
Using only [permitted source facts] and this voice playbook [rules], draft a short post, email and FAQ answer for [audience]. Adapt format without changing facts. Flag missing evidence instead of inventing claims or testimonials. Add a visual brief and suggested alt text. These are drafts for human approval; do not publish or schedule them.

TEST CASES
1. Voice: compare a generic draft with a version using your own permitted examples and explicit do/don't rules.
2. Evidence: include an unsupported statistic in a fictional brief. The editor must remove it or label it for verification before approval.
3. Publication: simulate account, date, time zone and status checks. A drafted item is not a scheduled or published item.

TRANSFER CHALLENGE
Have a peer edit one draft against the voice rules. Update the playbook and try a new topic; record whether review becomes easier without reducing accuracy.

SAVE AND REVIEW
Keep your own copy and follow the workbook submission instructions. Do not include passwords, private records or secret URLs.
