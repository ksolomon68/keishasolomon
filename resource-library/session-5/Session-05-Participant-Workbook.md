# Session 5: Brand voice and 30-day content plan

Brand Authority & Content Engines

## Your outcome

Create a voice playbook, adapt one supported idea into three channels, and plan a realistic 30-day editorial window without assuming daily publication.

### Before you begin

Bring two pieces of writing you own and may share, or use invented samples. Choose one audience, one goal, and three channels relevant to that audience. A document and spreadsheet are enough for the lab; no scheduling subscription is required.

### Working boundaries

Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

### What to keep

Keep a copy of your inputs, outputs, decisions and revisions. Browser-only drafts may not follow you to another device. Upload the completed resource or share a permitted link through your participant dashboard.

### Course coaching

Each cohort participant may request one remote coaching session during the course. Request it inside the platform; your instructor confirms the time. Use the same conversation for time changes.

## Lab guide

Follow your instructor or work through these steps using approved tools. A document or manual simulation is acceptable when a connected tool is unavailable; label its status honestly.

### Step 1

Build a voice playbook by answering: Who are we helping? What do they need? What do we sound like? Which phrases and claims must we avoid? Save examples, content pillars, and review rules.

### Step 2

Turn one source idea into three channel-specific drafts and a visual brief. Keep the underlying facts consistent; adapt the format and call to action to each channel.

### Step 3

Follow the six stages: voice brief, drafts, supporting visuals, checks and human approval, scheduling, destination verification. Build a 30-day draft calendar; simulate the scheduling steps unless real publication is explicitly approved.

### Worked example — fictional / illustrative

Fictional example: an invented workshop FAQ becomes a short post, an email draft and an FAQ answer. All three preserve the same supplied facts; no testimonial or performance claim is invented. A six-item schedule across 30 days may be more useful than 30 weak posts.
