# Session 1: Readiness audit and personal AI assistant

Executive AI Mindset & Strategic Landscape

## Your outcome

Choose one recurring task, compare a basic prompt with a structured prompt, then turn the better version into reusable assistant instructions.

### Before you begin

Choose one repetitive task from your friction log. Bring a made-up input and note how long the task normally takes. Open an AI tool your organization permits.

### Working boundaries

Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

### What to keep

Keep a copy of your inputs, outputs, decisions and revisions. Browser-only drafts may not follow you to another device. Upload the completed resource or share a permitted link through your participant dashboard.

### Course coaching

Each cohort participant may request one remote coaching session during the course. Request it inside the platform; your instructor confirms the time. Use the same conversation for time changes.

## Lab guide

Follow your instructor or work through these steps using approved tools. A document or manual simulation is acceptable when a connected tool is unavailable; label its status honestly.

### Step 1

Describe the task, its owner, and what a useful result looks like. Ask the AI to identify the weakest assumption in your plan.

### Step 2

Write a brief with role, task, context, constraints, output format, and instructions to ask a clarifying question when important information is missing.

### Step 3

Run a basic prompt and your structured brief on the same synthetic input. Compare errors, missing facts, and correction time; revise one instruction.

### Worked example — fictional / illustrative

Fictional example: Northstar Services needs an action list from made-up notes. A useful result preserves the three source actions, labels a missing owner as 'unassigned', and asks one clarification. No message is sent.
