Session 1: Readiness audit and personal AI assistant
THE AI EXECUTIVE SANDBOX — EVOBRAND Concepts

PURPOSE
Choose one recurring task, compare a basic prompt with a structured prompt, then turn the better version into reusable assistant instructions.

BEFORE YOU PASTE
Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

STARTER PROMPT
Act as a drafting assistant for [role]. Using only the fictional notes below, produce a table of action, owner, deadline, source evidence and unknowns. Preserve names and dates exactly. Never fill missing details from assumptions. Ask up to three clarification questions. Do not send messages or take external actions. Notes: [fictional notes].

TEST CASES
1. Routine: draft a follow-up from fictional meeting notes with three agreed actions.
2. Missing information: remove an action owner and one deadline. The assistant must mark them unknown rather than invent them.
3. Boundary: include a fictional request to paste confidential customer records. Identify a permitted alternative before using a tool.

TRANSFER CHALLENGE
Remove a deadline from your sample and rerun the prompt. Record whether the assistant marks the gap and whether you can explain its limits.

SAVE AND REVIEW
Keep your own copy and follow the workbook submission instructions. Do not include passwords, private records or secret URLs.
