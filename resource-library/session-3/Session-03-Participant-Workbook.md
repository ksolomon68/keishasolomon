# Session 3: Executive triage and time evidence

The 5-Hour Workweek Reclaim & Executive Workflows

## Your outcome

Build a repeatable triage workspace that turns fictional requests into a checked action list. Measure the complete task, including review.

### Before you begin

Choose a recurring triage task. Bring synthetic notes or emails and your baseline time. Include one ambiguous request.

### Working boundaries

Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

### What to keep

Keep a copy of your inputs, outputs, decisions and revisions. Browser-only drafts may not follow you to another device. Upload the completed resource or share a permitted link through your participant dashboard.

### Course coaching

Each cohort participant may request one remote coaching session during the course. Request it inside the platform; your instructor confirms the time. Use the same conversation for time changes.

## Lab guide

Follow your instructor or work through these steps using approved tools. A document or manual simulation is acceptable when a connected tool is unavailable; label its status honestly.

### Step 1

Define priority rules and the fields a useful action list needs. Interview yourself about the recurring decisions you currently make.

### Step 2

Save a reusable playbook with inputs, examples, output format, missing-data rules, and checks. Reopen it in a fresh task and confirm the assistant is using the current instructions.

### Step 3

Test a routine case, a missing-information case, and a conflicting case. Time the complete task including correction; update the playbook after the weakest result.

### Worked example — fictional / illustrative

Illustrative numbers only: a baseline task takes 30 minutes; AI preparation, execution, review and correction total 22 minutes. Net savings are 8 minutes per comparable run. If one run needs 35 minutes, report the 5-minute loss too. Do not treat this example as your result.
