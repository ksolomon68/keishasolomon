# Session 6: Prototype brief and usability test

Rapid Prototyping & Web Strategy

## Your outcome

Create one complete interactive path for a specific visitor goal, then improve the first confusing step using observed feedback.

### Before you begin

Choose one visitor goal and sketch the shortest route to it. Use fictional content and records; decide what the prototype must not collect or do.

### Working boundaries

Use fictional or explicitly approved examples. Do not paste private customer, employee, health or financial records into public AI tools. Follow your organization's tool and data rules. Treat outputs as drafts; a person verifies facts and approves external actions.

### What to keep

Keep a copy of your inputs, outputs, decisions and revisions. Browser-only drafts may not follow you to another device. Upload the completed resource or share a permitted link through your participant dashboard.

### Course coaching

Each cohort participant may request one remote coaching session during the course. Request it inside the platform; your instructor confirms the time. Use the same conversation for time changes.

## Lab guide

Follow your instructor or work through these steps using approved tools. A document or manual simulation is acceptable when a connected tool is unavailable; label its status honestly.

### Step 1

Define the visitor, one goal, and a three-step journey. Ask the assistant to challenge the scope and identify what can be tested without a full product.

### Step 2

Agree on a short plan, then build one interactive path with a primary action and success/error states. Review the result before adding features.

### Step 3

Ask someone to complete the task on a phone and with a keyboard. Record the first confusion, make one change, and repeat the test.

### Worked example — fictional / illustrative

Fictional example: a community workshop interest form uses sample names only and clearly states that it does not create a real registration. A tester misses the primary button; the team changes its label and placement, then repeats the task.
